About me

Research rigor, engineering habits.

I'm a first-year Master's student in Data Science at the University of Washington in Seattle. Before that I finished a B.Tech in Computer Science at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, ranking in the top 5% of a department of 1,900.

Most of my work sits where deep learning meets real constraints. At Samsung R&D I built a hybrid CNN that tells human photos apart from AI-generated ones and traces synthetic images back to the generator that made them. In medical imaging I've published on detecting diabetic retinopathy lesions in retinal scans. More recently I've been building multi-agent LLM systems with LangGraph, MCP and retrieval pipelines.

I also spent two years as a research assistant on fractional PDEs and Lie symmetry methods, and I have a competitive programming background including ICPC. I like problems where the math matters and the result has to run in production.
